## Block 1: Need (DRAFT / Continuity-safe), 66 words

> Mental and substance use disorders were the leading cause (23%) of total disease burden for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people in 2018, and 37% of First Nations adults reported a current mental health condition in 2018–19 (AIHW). For many First Nations people, good mental health is grounded in belonging, strong cultural identity, relationships and purpose, within a holistic view of social and emotional wellbeing (AIHW).

## Block 2: Focus and approach (DRAFT / Continuity-safe), 67 words

> MindPal is a digital wellbeing companion (daily check-in, journal, conversational companion), not a clinical treatment. In Stage 1 we are naming Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander people as a priority population. We have not yet co-designed with First Nations communities, and we will not adapt content, language or features for this population until First Nations people have shaped them, on governance and data terms agreed with them.

## Block 3: Complement, not replace (DRAFT / Continuity-safe), 60 words

> Strong First Nations–led resources already exist, such as WellMob, which brings together online social and emotional wellbeing resources "made by and for our mob" (Black Dog Institute). MindPal's role is to complement these and help people find them, not to duplicate or replace them. MindPal is not a crisis service. It directs people to 000 and Lifeline 13 11 14.

## Block 4: Staged plan (DRAFT / Continuity-safe), 59 words

> Stage 1 plan: (1) seek guidance from First Nations–led organisations and people with lived experience before designing; (2) agree governance and data terms, including who can pause the work; (3) run a paid co-design review of existing features; (4) add community-reviewed First Nations support listings to MindPal's help pathways; (5) publish no First Nations content without First Nations approval.
