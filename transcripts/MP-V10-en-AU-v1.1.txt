A reflection can be one sentence, one word, or nothing today. You do not need to explain everything that happened. You can keep this simple and finish whenever you want.

You might choose one question: What felt manageable? What was difficult? Or, what might I need next? Choose only one if it helps. You can also write something entirely different.

If writing makes you feel more stuck or distressed, stop. You could notice something around you, take a break, or reach out for human support. You do not have to turn this into a lesson.

You can reflect without typing anything into MindPal. Before saving personal information, check the privacy information for this version, including where entries are stored and who could access them. On a shared device or browser, someone else may be able to see saved entries. Before using any AI reflection feature, check what text would be sent and how it would be used. You can choose not to use it.
