A feeling does not need a perfect name. If you want to, choose a word that seems close enough: worried, sad, frustrated, tired, lonely, mixed, or not sure.

You can use your own word, or choose none. There is no need to explain what happened or tell a difficult story.

Perhaps try, I notice some worry. Or, I am not sure what this is. A word describes an experience; it is not a diagnosis.

You might instead ask what you need next: a quieter space, rest, or a safe person to speak with. Choose only what fits your circumstances.

If words make this harder, leave them aside. You can notice an ordinary object nearby or finish. Nothing needs to be written, saved or sent to AI.
