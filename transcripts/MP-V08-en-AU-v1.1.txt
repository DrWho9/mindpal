When you describe feeling anxious, you do not have to solve everything in one sitting. This short guide offers choices for everyday wellbeing; it cannot assess or diagnose what you are experiencing.

You might want a practical next step, a short external attention exercise, a break from this screen, or contact with someone who can support you. Breathing exercises are optional. You do not have to use them.

Physical symptoms can have different causes. MindPal cannot tell whether a symptom is anxiety. New, severe or concerning symptoms need medical help. In immediate danger or a medical emergency in Australia, call triple zero.

If worry or distress is affecting your daily life, you can discuss it with a qualified health professional. You do not need a label before asking for support.

For a conversation, you might prepare one question or mention one thing that has been difficult. You can decide what to share. You do not need to retell a painful event here.

You can finish now. You can open ‘Help me now’ for urgent-support options without completing a practice. MindPal does not monitor emergencies or summon help for you.
