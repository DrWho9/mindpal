Think about the next few minutes, rather than the whole day. Is there one small action that might be useful or meaningful? You can also choose rest.

Perhaps place one item where you need it, choose some music, or prepare a question for a person who supports you. Choose something that fits your situation.

You can make the action smaller, prepare without doing it, or decide against it. There is no score for finishing.

If pain, fatigue or your surroundings make it unsuitable, change the action or stop. You do not have to push through symptoms. New or severe symptoms may need medical assessment.

If you try something, you can ask whether it was manageable. The answer can be no. That information may help you choose differently, without judging yourself. You can finish here.
