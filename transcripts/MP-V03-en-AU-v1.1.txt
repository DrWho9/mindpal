This is an optional pause for everyday stress. You can stop at any time. If breathing exercises are uncomfortable for you, you can try noticing one neutral detail around you instead. You do not need to follow your breath.

If you would like to try, stay in a comfortable position. Let your breath remain at its ordinary depth and pace. No big breath, counting or holding is needed. Notice one breath coming and going, if that feels okay.

If focusing on your breathing brings dizziness, a sense of not getting enough air, discomfort or more panic, stop the exercise. You can shift attention to a neutral detail nearby using a sense that feels comfortable, or finish altogether.

You can stay with that detail briefly, or finish now. There is no need to make a feeling disappear. Seek medical advice for new, persistent or concerning physical symptoms. For severe difficulty breathing or another medical emergency in Australia, call triple zero. This exercise cannot tell you what is causing a symptom.

When you are ready, choose what is manageable next: rest, a small practical action, or support from a person. It is also okay to do nothing further here. You can return to the text guide whenever you want.
