You do not need to follow your breathing to take a moment. This is an optional way to notice something outside yourself.

Choose one neutral detail nearby, perhaps a shape, a colour or a quiet sound. Use whichever sense is comfortable for you, or skip this activity.

Notice one feature. You can pause the video for as long or as little as you want.

If noticing this way makes things harder, stop and choose something else. The aim is to offer a choice, not to guarantee a particular feeling. Rest or human support may be more useful. You can finish here.
