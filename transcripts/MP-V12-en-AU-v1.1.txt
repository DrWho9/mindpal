If you have questions about perimenopause—the transition leading up to menopause—menopause itself, or changes in mood or sleep, you can discuss them with a qualified health professional. You do not have to decide on the cause before asking for help.

MindPal cannot diagnose menopause or explain the cause of a mood, sleep or physical symptom. It does not recommend hormonal treatment, supplements or changes to medication.

You might prepare one or two questions for an appointment. For example: I have noticed a change in how I am feeling. What could we consider? Or, what support is available for what is affecting my day?

If notes are useful, keep them in a form that suits you. You could mention when a change started and what you would like help with. You do not have to complete a checklist or share intimate details with an app.

Several things may contribute to changes in mood or sleep. If low mood, anxiety or sleep problems persist or affect daily life, arrange a healthcare appointment rather than assuming they are part of menopause. New, severe or concerning physical symptoms also need medical assessment.

You can choose practical support for today, such as asking someone for help or making one demand smaller. That does not replace healthcare when you need it.

This pathway is optional, and you can leave it at any time. If writing or watching is unhelpful, stop.
